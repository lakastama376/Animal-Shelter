Part I: Terms of Service

Welcome to Tails of Freedom Animal Haven. By accessing or using our platform, creating an account, submitting an adoption application, or making a donation, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service.

1. Account Eligibility & Responsibilities

  • Account Creation: You must provide accurate, current, and complete information during registration, including your real name, valid phone number, and physical residential address.
  • Email Verification: Access to full system functionality requires verifying your email address via the link provided upon signup.
  • Account Security: You are responsible for safeguarding your credentials. Notify us immediately if you suspect unauthorized access to your account. Administrator accounts reserve the right to suspend or block users violating platform policies.

2. Adoption Process & Application Policy

All adoption submissions are subject to administrative review and screening:

  • Eligibility Screening: Completing the preliminary quiz or application form does not guarantee adoption approval.
  • Identity & Document Verification: Applicants must submit genuine government-issued IDs and valid proof of residence. Providing fraudulent documentation may lead to application rejection and account suspension.
  • Visitation & Final Approval: Approved applicants must attend scheduled shelter visitations. The shelter retains discretion to decline adoptions if a placement environment is deemed unsafe for the animal.

3. Mandatory Post-Adoption Weekly Monitoring

To ensure animal welfare, adopters commit to post-adoption check-ins:

  • Adopters must submit mandatory weekly updates, including photos, videos, and status notes, for a designated monitoring period (typically four consecutive weeks).
  • Failure to submit updates on time will flag the application as overdue. Persistent neglect of reporting or evidence of animal mistreatment may result in shelter intervention or legal action under applicable animal welfare laws.

4. Donations & Payment Processing

  • Payment Processing: Online transactions and donations are processed through PayMongo, supporting available payment methods such as QR Ph, e-wallets, and credit/debit cards.
  • Finality of Donations: Financial donations are voluntary and non-refundable once processed, subject to applicable law and payment-provider policies.
  • Payment Security: Payment credentials are handled through the payment provider's infrastructure and are not stored on our local servers, as applicable to the actual payment integration.

5. Pet Returns

If an adopter is no longer capable of caring for an adopted pet, they must initiate an official Return Request through the platform. Abandoning or rehoming the pet to an unauthorized third party without shelter consent is prohibited.

6. Incident Reporting & Community Standards

Users submitting rescue reports or incident alerts must provide accurate location details and factual information. Submitting false reports, harassing shelter personnel, or attempting to compromise platform security is prohibited.

7. Contact Information

For questions regarding these Terms of Service or shelter operations, please visit our Contact Page .

Part II: Privacy Policy

At Tails of Freedom Animal Haven, we are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you access or use our web application.

1. Information We Collect

To facilitate pet adoptions, user eligibility verification, and ongoing monitoring, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, username, contact number, and residential address.
  • Authentication Data: Login details processed through the authentication service, including social sign-in data when applicable.
  • Adoption Application Data: Personal details, housing conditions, proof of residence, government-issued IDs, and screening questionnaire responses.
  • Post-Adoption Monitoring Media: Photos, videos, and status updates submitted during post-adoption check-ins.
  • Technical & Usage Information: Session tokens, IP addresses, audit logs, and interaction records necessary for system security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for operational and animal welfare purposes, including:

  • Evaluating and processing pet adoption applications.
  • Verifying applicant identities and contact information.
  • Tracking post-adoption monitoring reports.
  • Sending account updates, adoption status notifications, and visitation schedules.
  • Maintaining system security, audit trails, and preventing fraudulent applications.

3. Data Storage & Security

We apply administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information:

  • Database Storage: User records and adoption document metadata are stored in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • File & Media Storage: Uploaded identification documents, proof of address, and monitoring photos/videos are stored through the configured cloud storage infrastructure.
  • Access Controls: Sensitive applicant data, including government IDs, is restricted to authorized administrative personnel.

Important: Users should maintain strong passwords and keep their account credentials confidential.

4. User Rights & Data Retention

You may review, update, or request correction of your personal details through your Profile settings. Adoption records and audit logs are retained only for as long as necessary for operational purposes, animal protection history, and applicable legal obligations.

5. Contact Information

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact .
